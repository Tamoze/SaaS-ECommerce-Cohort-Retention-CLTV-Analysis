## Project Overview

Customer retention is a critical driver of sustainable business growth. While acquiring new customers fuels expansion, retaining existing customers significantly improves profitability and long-term customer value.

This project applies **Cohort Analysis** and **Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)** modeling to analyze customer retention patterns, identify churn behavior, and estimate customer value over time using transactional data.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end business analytics workflow, from data cleaning and cohort creation to executive insights and strategic recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

Many organizations focus on acquiring new customers but fail to understand how well those customers are retained over time. Without visibility into customer retention and lifetime value, businesses struggle to:

- Reduce customer churn
- Improve customer engagement
- Optimize marketing investment
- Forecast future revenue
- Increase long-term profitability

This project addresses these challenges by analyzing customer cohorts and estimating Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).

---

## Project Objectives

- Perform customer cohort analysis.
- Calculate monthly customer retention rates.
- Identify customer churn patterns.
- Estimate historical Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).
- Segment customers based on purchasing behavior.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

---

## Business Questions

This analysis seeks to answer questions such as:

- How well are customers retained after their first purchase?
- Which customer cohorts generate the highest long-term value?
- During which month does customer churn peak?
- How does retention vary across customer segments?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue over time?
