# Executive Summary

Customer retention is one of the strongest indicators of sustainable business growth. Although many organizations invest heavily in customer acquisition, long-term profitability depends on understanding customer behavior after acquisition.

This project applies Cohort Analysis and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) modeling to analyze customer retention patterns, identify churn behavior, and estimate customer value over time using transactional data.

The insights generated will support strategic decision-making in customer retention, marketing optimization, and revenue forecasting.

---

# Business Problem

Acquiring new customers can cost several times more than retaining existing ones. Many businesses monitor acquisition metrics but fail to understand how long customers remain active or how much value they generate throughout their relationship with the business.

Without a clear understanding of customer retention and lifetime value, organizations risk inefficient marketing spending, declining profitability, and increased customer churn.

This project addresses these challenges by analyzing customer cohorts and estimating Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) to support data-driven business decisions.

---

# Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Analyze customer retention using cohort analysis.
- Measure monthly customer retention rates.
- Identify customer churn patterns.
- Calculate historical Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).
- Segment customers based on purchasing behavior and value.
- Generate actionable recommendations to improve customer retention and profitability.
