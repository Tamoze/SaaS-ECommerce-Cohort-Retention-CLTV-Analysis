
# Week 3 Project Progress Report

## Executive Summary

Week 3 focused on transforming customer transaction data into actionable business intelligence through Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) analysis. The work involved engineering customer-level analytical features, calculating historical CLTV, segmenting customers based on value, and developing executive-ready reports and visualizations. These activities established a comprehensive understanding of customer behaviour and long-term business value, providing the analytical foundation for predictive modelling and dashboard development in Week 4.

## Objectives Achieved

Successfully completed the development of the customer-level analytical dataset and implemented Historical Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) analysis. Key customer metrics, including Average Order Value, Purchase Frequency, Customer Lifespan, and Customer Age, were engineered to evaluate purchasing behaviour and estimate customer value over time. Customers were segmented into High Value, Medium Value, and Low Value groups, enabling strategic customer profiling and targeted business insights.

An executive reporting layer was also developed by creating KPI summary tables, identifying the highest-value customers, producing professional visualizations, and documenting strategic business recommendations. These deliverables transformed the analytical outputs into decision-support assets suitable for executive reporting and future dashboard integration.

## Activities Completed

### Customer Analytics

- Created a customer-level analytical dataset.
- Calculated Average Order Value (AOV).
- Calculated Purchase Frequency.
- Calculated Customer Lifespan.
- Calculated Customer Age.
- Computed Historical Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).

### Customer Segmentation

- Classified customers into High Value, Medium Value, and Low Value segments.
- Generated customer segment distribution statistics.
- Calculated average revenue, average orders, and average CLTV for each segment.

### Executive Reporting

- Developed an Executive KPI Summary.
- Identified the Top 10 Customers by Historical CLTV.
- Generated five executive-quality visualizations:
  - Customer Segment Distribution
  - Average Revenue by Customer Segment
  - Average Orders by Customer Segment
  - Average Historical CLTV by Customer Segment
  - Top 10 Customers by Historical CLTV
- Produced an Executive Business Report containing strategic recommendations.

### Project Outputs

Generated and exported:

- Customer Summary Dataset
- Executive KPI Summary
- Top 10 CLTV Customers
- Executive Business Report
- Five analytical visualizations
- Supporting processed datasets for dashboard development

## Key Findings

- Analysed 5,878 unique customers.
- Generated customer insights from 36,969 completed orders.
- Analysed approximately $17.37 million in total revenue.
- Calculated an average Historical CLTV of approximately $1,895.15 per customer.
- Identified Medium Value customers (46.02%) as the largest customer segment, representing the greatest opportunity for customer growth initiatives.
- Confirmed that High Value customers contribute disproportionately to long-term business value and should be prioritized for retention strategies.

## Business Impact

The analyses completed during Week 3 provide actionable insights into customer purchasing behaviour and long-term customer value. The resulting customer segmentation supports personalized marketing, customer retention initiatives, and more efficient allocation of marketing resources. The executive summaries and visualizations also establish a strong foundation for business intelligence reporting and data-driven decision-making.
