
# Executive Business Report

## Executive Summary

This analysis evaluated customer purchasing behaviour and Historical Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) using transactional data from an online retail business. Customers were segmented into High Value, Medium Value, and Low Value groups based on their historical contribution to business revenue.

## Key Findings

- Total Customers: 5,878
- Total Revenue: $17,374,804.27
- Total Orders: 36,969
- Average Historical CLTV: $1,895.15
- Largest Segment: Medium Value Customers (46.02%)

## Strategic Recommendations

- Retain High Value customers.
- Grow Medium Value customers.
- Reactivate Low Value customers.
- Incorporate CLTV into marketing decisions.
